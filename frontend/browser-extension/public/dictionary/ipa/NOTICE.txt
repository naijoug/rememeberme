Packaged English IPA pronunciation data

Source: https://github.com/open-dict-data/ipa-dict
Copyright (c) 2016 dohliam, MIT except for third-party data identified below.

US data is based on a modified version of cmudict-ipa by Lingliang Zhang
(https://github.com/lingz/cmudict-ipa), with stress markers using syllabify
by Kyle Gorman (https://github.com/kylebgorman/syllabify), MIT.
See ipa-dict-LICENSE.txt, en-US-LICENSE.txt, cmudict-LICENSE.txt and
syllabify-LICENSE.txt for permission and copyright notices.

UK data is derived from ipacards by leoboiko / melissaboiko
(https://github.com/melissaboiko/ipacards), GPL version 3.
The UK-derived data remains under that license; see en-UK-LICENSE.txt.

Remember Me modifications, 2026-10-10: filter to supported single-word
spellings, normalize lookup keys to lowercase, and group unchanged IPA
strings into 64 JSON shards. No pronunciation text is generated or corrected.
Original corresponding TSV sources are shipped as en_US.txt.gz and
en_UK.txt.gz. Source checksums and coverage are in metadata.json.
The reproducible converter is scripts/build-ipa.py in the Remember Me source.

The dictionaries do not endorse Remember Me. IPA variants are not selected
by sense or context; browser speech voices may pronounce a different variant.
